Wednesday, November 11, 2026
Scaling and Local Engagement: Dynamic Outcomes of Coalescing Local Activities

CHAT for Change · Thematic Group Seminar

Aydin Bal and Maria Spante will explore scaling and local engagement through coalescing as a process through which locally situated activities connect and develop a broader shared direction while retaining their distinct local conditions and practices. This opens up ways of understanding scaling as an emergent process and how this unfolds in research.

Drawing on examples from their own research, they will connect the theoretical discussion to research practice. The first hour combines presentations and conversation, followed by 30 minutes of open-floor discussion.

Speakers: Aydin Bal and Maria Spante, PhD

Time: 9:00–10:30 a.m. Wisconsin–Madison (CST) / 16:00–17:30 Sweden (CET)

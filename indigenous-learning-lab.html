Indigenous Learning Lab

A dedicated space for the Indigenous Learning Lab story, developed with attention to community history, knowledge, relationships, and consent.

Tell the story with the community, not only about the community.

Origins, relationships, and responsibilities

The Indigenous Learning Lab deserves a dedicated place in the Learning Lab story. This page should document its origins, the people and communities who shaped it, the questions that guided the work, and what the experience contributed to the wider methodology.

The existing CRPBIS website has been identified as a source for Indigenous Learning Lab content, but its page was not accessible during the initial review. For that reason, this prototype does not invent project-specific details.

Content to develop with collaborators

  • Community-defined account of how the work began.
  • Historical and cultural context, reviewed by the relevant community partners.
  • People and organizations involved, with agreed-upon naming and attribution.
  • Research outputs, reflections, and lessons that partners wish to share.
  • Guidance on what should remain private or not be represented publicly.

Please review this page with Indigenous collaborators before publication. Avoid representing Indigenous Nations as interchangeable or as a single geographic point.

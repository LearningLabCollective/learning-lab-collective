Learning Lab Methodology

A collaborative process for understanding systemic challenges and developing new forms of collective activity.

This is an introductory summary, not a substitute for the full methodology and its source citations.

A collaborative process for systemic change

The CRPBIS materials describe Learning Lab as an inclusive problem-solving activity grounded in Cultural-Historical Activity Theory (CHAT). In this approach, complex challenges are explored in relation to the histories, tools, rules, roles, relationships, and purposes that shape collective activity.

Rather than treating a problem as the fault of an individual, participants examine tensions and contradictions in the broader system, then work together to develop and test new possibilities. The precise activities and sequence should be adapted to each context.

Our Story

Explore the people, places, and questions that shaped Learning Lab—and continue to shape its future.

Every methodology has a history—and histories are shaped by people and place.

Tracing the story

Learning Lab's story should be told through the people, relationships, questions, and community contexts that shaped its development. CRPBIS is an important source for documenting part of that history and the methodology's development.

The new global website treats this history as a beginning, not the boundary of the community. It should acknowledge original research, community partners, and the contributions of Indigenous collaborators while making the evolution of the work understandable to new audiences.

01

Where questions emerged

Describe the context and challenges that led to the development of the approach.

02

Learning with communities

Document how people and partnerships shaped the work and its principles.

03

Learning across contexts

Show how the approach has been adapted, questioned, and extended in different settings.
